# Phase 4. What we need from you to open the app stores.

Dr. Hinton, this is the list of accounts only you can open. Apple and
Google both require the account to belong to the business that owns the
app, not to us. Once these are open, we build, test, and submit. Nothing
in Phase 4 moves until items 1 and 2 exist.

## Why now

You already have a working practice on the web. The home screen install
covers most people. What it does not cover is the App Store and Google
Play listing itself, the icon on a stranger's phone before they have
ever heard of Joy Up, and the trust that comes from a real store page
with your name on it. That is what Phase 4 buys.

The account cost is small and one time per year. Apple is ninety nine
dollars a year. Google is twenty five dollars, paid once, ever. Both
together are one hundred twenty four dollars for the first year.
Everything after that is our build time, which we priced separately.
The one hundred twenty four dollars is not our fee. It is the toll
Apple and Google charge to publish anything at all, and it has to be
paid by Kuumba, LLC directly so the app is owned by your company, not
by us or by any one person.

Open these now and the accounts will be ready and verified by the time
we have a build to submit. Apple in particular can take two to three
weeks to verify a business account, so starting early is the whole point.

## 1. Apple Developer Program. Ninety nine dollars a year.

Go to developer.apple.com/programs and enroll.

Choose **Organization**, not Individual. An organization account puts
Kuumba, LLC on the App Store listing as the seller of record, and it
lets more than one of us manage the account later without sharing your
personal Apple ID password. Individual accounts show your personal name
as the seller, which is not what you want for a company product.

You will need:

- Your legal business name, exactly as it appears on your paperwork with
  the state. Kuumba, LLC.
- A D-U-N-S (Data Universal Numbering System) number for the business.
  See item 3 below. Apple will not let you finish enrollment as an
  organization without one.
- Someone with legal authority to bind the company. That is you, or
  whoever holds that role at Kuumba, LLC.
- A company phone number that can be answered during business hours.
  Apple calls to verify.

## 2. Google Play Console. Twenty five dollars, one time.

Go to play.google.com/console/signup and enroll.

Choose the account type that matches a company, not a personal account,
and enter Kuumba, LLC as the developer name, not your personal name.
Google does not require a D-U-N-S number and verifies faster than Apple,
usually within a couple of days.

Same rule as Apple. This has to be a Kuumba, LLC account, not a personal
Google account you already use for something else. Keeping the app under
the company account means the app stays with the business if anything
about our arrangement ever changes, and it keeps your personal Google
account separate from the business one.

## 3. D-U-N-S number. Free. Takes about two weeks.

This is only needed because of item 1, the Apple organization account.

Go to Dun and Bradstreet at dnb.com and request a D-U-N-S number for
Kuumba, LLC if the business does not already have one. Search first,
some businesses already have one assigned without knowing it. The
request itself is free. Approval commonly takes one to two weeks, so
this is the item to start today, before anything else on this list,
since everything with Apple is stuck behind it.

## 4. Tax information (banking is optional)

Both stores ask for tax information once your account is open.

- KuumbaPro is a free app with no in-app purchases planned for v1.0.
  That means **Apple and Google do not require paid-app banking setup**
  for us to publish. You do not need to have a Kuumba, LLC bank account
  ready before enrolling. Do not stall the enrollment waiting on
  bank paperwork.
- Both stores will still walk you through a short tax questionnaire.
  Have Kuumba, LLC's EIN (Employer Identification Number, the federal
  tax ID) on hand for that step. A US LLC filling this in is the simple,
  common case.
- If we later decide to add paid content in Phase 3, that is when Apple
  and Google will ask for a business bank account and routing number,
  and we will handle it then, not now.

## 5. App Store Connect, once your Apple account is approved

Log in at appstoreconnect.apple.com and create a new app record. We
will send you the exact app name, the icon file, and the screenshots to
upload, along with the store description and keywords already written
in your voice. Your part is uploading what we send and clicking through
the app record screens. This takes about fifteen minutes once the files
are in hand.

## 6. Google Play Console, once your Google account is approved

Same as item 5, in the Play Console instead. You will also fill out a
content rating questionnaire and a Data Safety declaration. Both are
short sets of yes or no questions about what the app contains and what
data it collects. KuumbaPro has no violence, no mature content, no user
generated content, and no data collection in v1.0, so both questionnaires
land quickly at the lowest rating and a clean data safety section.

## 9. What to send back to us

Once items 1 and 2 are open, send us three things so we can build and
submit on your behalf without needing your password for either account.

1. **Apple Team ID.** A ten character code shown on your Apple Developer
   account page, under Membership.
2. **Android signing SHA-256 fingerprint.** We generate this one
   together once your Play Console account exists. You do not need to
   find it yourself.
3. **App Store Connect API key and issuer ID.** Also generated from
   inside your Apple account, under Users and Access, then Keys. This is
   what lets us upload builds automatically later instead of you
   uploading each one by hand.

We will walk you through generating all three when the time comes.
Nothing above requires technical knowledge beyond clicking the button we
point you to.
