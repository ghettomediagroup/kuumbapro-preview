Book a discovery call

A short conversation about what your people are carrying and what you need them to be able to do afterward. No charge, and nothing is scoped or quoted until Dr. Hinton understands the need.

Why there is no price list

A keynote for four hundred people at a symposium and a standing session for a team of nine are not the same work, and pricing them off a menu would mean guessing at one of them.

Every engagement is built for the room it is going into. Fees are set directly with you, against what the work actually requires. You will have a number before anything is committed.

What the call covers

  • What your people are carrying right now
  • What you need them to be able to do afterward
  • Format, length, and whether it is in person or virtual
  • Timing, and what is realistic
  • What the engagement would cost

If it is not a fit, he will say so on the call.
