About this session

This session sits with Joy as the first principle in JEBBEDIAH. Not joy as a mood to chase. Joy as a rhythm you return to, on purpose, at a pace you can keep.

Dr. Harvey Hinton III walks through the Joy Up 30 Second Joy Reset (TM) as a small, repeatable practice you can carry into an ordinary day. He is not asking you to feel a certain way. He is inviting you to try a shape.

By the end, you will know what the reset looks like when JEBBEDIAH does it, and you will have a version of it to try on your own.

Reflection questions

  1. What does joy look like, plainly, in your week so far?
  2. Where in your day would a 30 second pause be easiest to keep?
  3. What would change if you treated joy as a rhythm rather than a reward?
  4. Whose company reminds you of the version of yourself you like being?
  5. What is one small thing you would like to return to on purpose this week?
