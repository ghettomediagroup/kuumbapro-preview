Questions people ask.

What is Joy Up (TM)?

Joy Up (TM) is a general wellness practice created by Dr. Harvey Hinton III. It is the entry point into the JEBBEDIAH Way, his nine-principle framework for navigating complex social systems. It is not medical or mental health care.

Is this Laughter Yoga?

No. Dr. Hinton is a Certified Laughter Yoga Leader, and the practice draws on training from that lineage. Laughter Yoga is a trademark of Laughter Yoga International, founded by Dr. Madan Kataria. Joy Up (TM) is a separate practice, created by Dr. Hinton.

What actually happens on the live drop-in?

Thirty minutes on Zoom. A short grounding, the week's JEBBEDIAH micro-practice, and space to sit with it together. Cameras and mics are optional. The session is recorded and added to the library.

Do I have to show up live?

No. Every live session is recorded and posted to the on-demand library, usually within a day. The membership is built to work for people who can join live and for people who can only listen back later.

Does Dr. Hinton answer questions personally?

One member question is answered by Dr. Hinton each month, in writing, and posted in the members area for everyone to read. Questions are selected based on what will be most useful to the whole group, not on order received.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Membership is month to month. You can cancel any time from your account. Access continues through the end of the month you have already paid for.
