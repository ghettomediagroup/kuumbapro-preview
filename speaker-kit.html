Bio, three lengths

One line

Dr. Harvey Hinton III is a thought partner, keynote speaker, and facilitator. He is the founder of Kuumba, LLC and the creator of Community Engaged Healing© and the Joy Up 30 Second Joy Reset™.

Short

Dr. Harvey Hinton III is a national thought leader with over 20 years of experience advancing leadership, wellness, and social impact across higher education, nonprofit, and corporate sectors. A Robert Wood Johnson Foundation Culture of Health Leaders fellow, he is the Founder and Chief Change Agent of Kuumba, LLC and a facilitator with the Center for Creative Leadership.

Full

Dr. Harvey Hinton III is a native of Durham, North Carolina, rooted in a rich tradition of Black leaders, educators, athletes, builders, and healers. He earned his PhD and MS in Curriculum and Instruction from Purdue University and a BS in Industrial Technology from North Carolina A&T State University. He is recognized for translating research and lived experience into practical frameworks that support learning, self-discovery, and applied leadership growth, including his signature framework, Community Engaged Healing©, and the JEBBEDIAH Way, nine principles for navigating complex social systems.
