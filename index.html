Joy is the entryway.

The Joy Up 30 Second Joy Reset™ is a self-regulation practice. You can do it anytime. It is the entry point into the JEBBEDIAH Way, my framework of nine principles for navigating complex social systems.

“There is no perfect way to do it. Participation matters more than precision. Even partial engagement—one breath, one pause—is enough to begin.”

Joy Up 30 Second Joy Reset™

Five steps. Go at your own pace.

30seconds
  1. Ground Place one hand on your chest and one on your belly. Physical contact signals orientation and safety.
    If that feels uncomfortable in public, adapt:
    • rest your hands on your thighs
    • press your feet gently into the floor
    • notice where your body is supported
    • hold something steady (a pen, a cup, a chair)
    • take one slow look around the room

    Grounding is about contact, not posture.

  2. Breathe Inhale slowly through your nose. No forcing. No counting. Let the breath arrive as it is.
    A single intentional inhale is enough.
  3. Smile Allow a gentle, internal smile. This is not a social performance. It is a signal—small, subtle—reminding you that you are not under threat right now.
    If smiling feels inaccessible, soften your jaw or relax your tongue.
  4. Laugh Begin with a soft, intentional laugh. It may feel awkward or quiet. That is fine. Laughter is a physical act, and people often notice their body loosening before their thinking does.
    Silent or internal laughter is also valid.
  5. Release Exhale fully. Drop your shoulders. Let the body settle.
    Notice what is different, even slightly.

“You are not chasing joy. You are noticing access.”

With practice, these steps become less something you do and more something you return to.

“There’s no right way to laugh. There’s no wrong way to laugh. You just laugh.” — Dr. Harvey Hinton III
