## The Joy Up practice, right on the page

Anyone visiting the site can do the practice without downloading
anything, without signing up, and without paying. Five steps, thirty
seconds, self paced. The ring at the top counts thirty seconds. You
tap Next to move through Ground, Breathe, Smile, Laugh, Release.

Two things worth knowing.

1. If someone lands on `/joy-up` or clicks a link like
   `kuumbapro.org/#begin`, the reset opens and starts on its own.
   That is the address to share on a card, on a stage, on an intro.
2. When someone finishes, they see a Share button. On a phone, that
   opens the native share sheet. On a laptop, it copies the link.
   Nothing tracks who shared or where.

## The Joy Up app, as a web install

You asked about apps for iPhone and Android. The right first step for
your money is not a store submission. It is what you have now.

Anyone visiting your homepage on their phone can add Joy Up to their
home screen as an app. On iPhone Safari, they tap Share and pick Add
to Home Screen. On Android Chrome, three dots and Install App. The
result is a Joy Up icon on their phone that opens straight into the
practice, works without internet, and never has a browser bar. The
homepage has the walkthrough.

That covers most of what a native app would do for you, without a fee
per year and without the review process. When you want the full
native app, with push notifications, wearable integrations, and the
Apple and Google store listings, that is a separate project we can
line up later.

## The guide

version out in the world had a broken text layer, so every "fi" and

fixed. The trademark is written the same way in every place. The
copyright line is Kuumba, LLC, 2026. The pages are unchanged
otherwise.
